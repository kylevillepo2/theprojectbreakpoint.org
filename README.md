# 🎾 Project Breakpoint

A modern, responsive website for Project Breakpoint - a tennis non-profit organization providing free tennis programs and mentorship to underserved youth in Fullerton and San Diego.

## 🌟 About Project Breakpoint

Project Breakpoint is dedicated to breaking down barriers through tennis, providing free tennis instruction and mentorship to underserved youth. Our mission is to create pathways to college scholarships and lifelong success through the sport of tennis.

### Our Impact
- **200+ Kids Served** across Fullerton and San Diego
- **100% Free Programs** - no cost to participants
- **Professional Coaching** and mentorship
- **College Pathways** through tennis scholarships

## 🚀 Website Features

### 🏠 **Home Page**
- Compelling mission statement with impact statistics
- Core values: Accessibility, Excellence, and Community
- Direct links to events and donation pages

### 👥 **About Page**
- Organization story and mission
- Impact statistics and team information
- Modern, responsive design

### 📅 **Events Page**
- Current volunteer opportunities in Fullerton
- Email notification signup for program updates
- Partnership inquiry forms

### 💰 **Donation Page**
- Venmo integration with QR code scanning
- Impact stories and transparent funding breakdown
- Multiple donation amounts and custom options

### 📞 **Contact Page**
- Contact form with service area information
- Multiple contact methods for Fullerton, San Diego, and Orange County

## 🛠️ Technology

Built with modern web technologies:
- **React.js** for the frontend
- **Tailwind CSS** for styling
- **MongoDB** for contact form storage
- **Vercel** for hosting and deployment
